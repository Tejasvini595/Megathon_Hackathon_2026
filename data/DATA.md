# Green Home Twin dataset

Real EPC certificates for **129,318 existing homes in 10 councils** (England and Wales), augmented with real sales, real postcode-level metered consumption, and a documented simulator for the parts no public dataset provides (monthly meter readings, behaviour, retrofit outcomes).

- **Build:** `uv run python build.py` (seed 42, deterministic, about 3 minutes), then `uv run python validate.py`.
- **Outputs:** `out/` (shippable; Parquet + CSV). `hidden/` is for evaluation only and must never be used as model features.
- **Validation:** `out/validation_report.md` (20/20 checks pass). Build statistics: `out/build_log.json`.
- **Sources and licences:** `sources.md`.

> Agreement with simulated targets does not establish real-world accuracy. Every simulated field is labelled, and the rules that produce it are disclosed below.

## 2. Selection and filtering

1. All 143,257 certificates lodged 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-30 for Barnet, Birmingham, Bristol, Camden, Cardiff, City of London, Croydon, Liverpool, Manchester and Newcastle upon Tyne.
2. Keep existing dwellings only: RdSAP reports, excluding "New dwelling". New-build certificates are design-stage assessments of empty homes with no usage history.
3. One certificate per UPRN (the latest), so one home = one row. 2,448 homes without a UPRN are kept with `flag_no_uprn`.
4. Result: **129,318 homes**: C 78,267 · D 39,032 · B 6,573 · E 4,178 · F 651 · A 384 · G 233.
5. Natural quality flags (not dropped): `flag_tiny_floor_area` (< 20 m²), `flag_rooms_implausible`, `flag_missing_tenure`, `flag_missing_photo_supply`.

**Known bias:** EPCs are lodged on sale, letting or survey, so private and social rentals and flats are over-represented compared with the whole housing stock. There are no rural homes, and no Scotland or Northern Ireland.

## 3. Simulated monthly energy (`energy_usage`)

Period Oct 2024 – Sep 2026 (24 months). Columns: `electricity_kwh` (grid import), `gas_kwh`, `heat_network_kwh`, `other_fuel_kwh` (oil/LPG/solid), `solar_generation_kwh`, `source`.

For each home:

1. **EPC demand.** Delivered kWh per end use (space heating, hot water, lighting) and fuel = EPC cost ÷ SAP 10.2 fuel price. The total is then rescaled so that Σ delivered × SAP primary energy factor equals the EPC's own primary energy (`energy_consumption_current × floor area`). Costs only set the split between end uses; the EPC primary energy sets the magnitude.
2. **Performance gap by band.** Space heating × {A/B 1.00, C 0.92, D 0.81, E 0.68, F 0.55, G 0.52}, because EPCs over-predict, more so for poor bands (Few et al. 2023: ~8% for C, ~48% for F/G; D and E interpolated). Hot water × 0.90.
3. **Hidden behaviour** (`hidden/hidden_state`):
    - setpoint ~ N(19.5, 1.2) °C, at 10% heating per °C;
    - occupancy multiplier, lognormal sd 0.20;
    - appliance multiplier, sd 0.25;
    - 0.5% of homes vacant (× 0.15);
    - for walls recorded as "no insulation (assumed)", 15% are actually insulated (heating × 0.88). This puts the performance gap where the assessor did not inspect.
4. **Non-SAP electricity:** appliances = 2,050 × (floor area / 80)^0.35 kWh. Gas cooking 300 kWh for half of gas homes.
5. **Real local anchoring:** annual gas and electricity are partially pooled toward the **real DESNZ 2024 postcode-level median** (outcode median where a postcode is suppressed). The postcode ratio is shrunk toward the outcode ratio with weight n/(n+5), and the scale applied is ratio^0.5. Partial pooling is used because the EPC population differs in composition from all meters in a postcode.
6. **Solar:** generation = kWp × regional yield (740–830 kWh/kWp). 45% is self-consumed (capped at half of demand) and reduces grid import; exports are excluded.
7. **Monthly shape:** space heating ∝ that month's regional heating degree days (Met Office HadUK-Grid, Hitchin formula, base 15.5 °C). Hot water, lighting and appliances follow mild cosine seasonality. Monthly noise is lognormal sd 0.05.
8. **Faults** (every one in `hidden/fault_log`):
    - random missing months (4%);
    - long gaps of 3–8 months (3% of homes);
    - short histories of 2–5 summer months only (2%), which are below the 6-month / winter threshold → "unavailable";
    - EPC records PV but no generation (5% of PV homes);
    - windows description blanked (1%).

    **Missing readings are NaN rows, never zero.** Zero means the home has no such supply.

**Annualisation guidance for the engine:** annualise from available months with degree-day adjustment. Report "unavailable" if there are fewer than 6 months or no winter month (Dec–Feb).

Validation (`out/validation_report.md`):
- median gas 10,107 kWh vs NEED 2026 10,000;
- gas-heated homes' electricity 2,228 vs NEED 2,500 (all homes);
- Spearman correlation with real DESNZ postcode medians: gas 0.79, electricity 0.69;
- corr(SAP, simulated kWh/m²) = −0.34 vs −0.73 for EPC modelled intensity, so the consumption sub-score is not a copy of the rating.

## 5. Labels (reference targets)

| File | Content | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `labels_retrofit` | Per home × retrofit: `feasible` (hard rules), per-fuel saving when applied alone (negative = increase), `relevance` (High ≥10% of baseline E, Medium ≥3%, Low), CO2, `epc_recommended` | Simulator on the hidden true state + real EPC recommendations |
| `labels_package` | Fabric-first package (heat pump replaces boiler upgrade where feasible): per-fuel before/after, `improvement_potential_pct` = 100 × (E_before − E_after) / E_before with E = grid import + gas; a no-heat-pump variant; ranks; `epc_potential_improvement_pct` (real RdSAP package result) | Simulator / real |
| `labels_package_sequence` | Marginal saving of each measure in sequence | Simulator |
| `labels_value` | rating_before → rating_after (EPC potential), uplift % low/mid/high and £ | Rating premium fitted on **5,187 real sales with a known rating at sale** |

**Retrofit simulator rules** (fractions of space heating unless stated; `out/retrofit_catalogue`):
- loft 15% / 8% / 3% (from 0 / < 100 / 100–199 mm);
- cavity 12% (none) / 6% (partial);
- solid wall 22% / 10% (≈ NEED's 17% whole-house gas median);
- floor 6% (suspended) / 4% (solid);
- glazing 10% / 7% / 4%;
- smart controls 6% × behaviour factor (larger where the hidden setpoint is high);
- condensing boiler: efficiency 0.72 → 0.85;
- heat pump: useful heat ÷ SCOP 2.8 (hot water 2.5), gas removed, electricity rises;
- solar PV 2.5 kWp.

Wall insulation on homes whose walls are secretly already insulated saves only 2–3%. The label therefore knows something the EPC-based engine cannot. Costs are the median EPC indicative cost for the mapped measures.

**Heat pumps and improvement potential:** switching gas to a heat pump at SCOP 2.8 cuts purchased kWh by ~57% (median), well above the brief's illustrative 15–25%, because kWh are counted equally across fuels. Rankings using the package are therefore dominated by heat-pump eligibility. `improvement_potential_pct_no_heat_pump` (median 14%) is provided for fabric-led ranking, and it correlates with the real EPC potential improvement (Spearman 0.69).

**Value uplift:** OLS of log(HPI-indexed price) on SAP at sale (per 10 points), with controls for log floor area, property type, age band, Land Registry type and outcode fixed effects (HC1 standard errors). Result: +4.2% per 10 SAP points, 95% CI 3.1–5.3%. Uplift range = the CI applied to (potential SAP − current SAP). **Associational, not causal:** improved homes may have had other renovations.

**Value model:** LightGBM over location, type, size, age and SAP. GroupKFold by postcode gives median APE 12.4%, vs 16.8% for an outcode £/m² baseline. Matched sales that deviate from the model by more than 3× are rejected (28 cases). Our median values are below the UK HPI *average* (mean) prices because the EPC population skews to flats and rentals.

## 6. Splits and leakage

- `splits`: 70/15/15 grouped by postcode (no postcode spans two splits), plus **Liverpool held out entirely** (`geo_test`, 19,131 homes).
- **Not available at prediction time** (never use as features): everything in `hidden/`, all `labels_*`, `sales_history` rows after the prediction date, `value_source` / `current_value_estimate` when predicting value, `potential_*` fields when predicting post-retrofit ratings.
- `current_value_estimate` (baseline, valuation 2026-07) is separate from `sales_history.price` (historical).

## 7. Privacy

- Shipped files carry `property_id` only. UPRN and street addresses exist only in `hidden/crosswalk_uprn_address`, used for the Land Registry match.
- Simulated meter readings are tagged so they cannot be mistaken for a real household's usage.
- No personal or customer data is used.

## 8. Limitations

- Meter data is simulated (anchored on real homes and real postcode medians), not real customer data.
- Several constants are approximate: SAP prices/PEFs, PV yields, SCOP, and NEED medians for cavity/loft/boiler savings. Each is tagged `[approx]` in `gen/constants.py`.
- Only 19% of homes match a sale, so 81% of current values are model estimates. Most past sales have no known rating at sale, because the EPC extract covers 2025–26 only.
- Relevance labels from EPC recommendations are rule-based, computed from the same fields we use as features.
- Condensing-boiler status is inferred from the EPC efficiency rating, and almost all gas boilers are rated Good, so the boiler upgrade is rarely feasible.
